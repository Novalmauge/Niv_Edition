I've been immersed in D&D for over two decades - ever since I stumbled upon my father's vintage collection – back when Elves and Dwarves were classes, not races!  With this, I bring a deep understanding of the game's evolution to the table.

I run my games on FoundryVTT, a platform that's both powerful and free for players. While I'm comfortable with theater of the mind, I also utilize maps for those who crave a more visual and tactical experience.

My passion lies in crafting RP games with dynamic combat.  Think engaging roleplay with a focus on a blooming narrative, but don't worry about the lack of crunchy combat.

Whether you're a seasoned adventurer or a wide-eyed newcomer, I'm excited to welcome you to my table.  I love fostering a fun and inclusive environment where everyone feels comfortable contributing to the story.
