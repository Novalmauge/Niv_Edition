# Companions: Sidekicks, Cohorts, and Hirelings

While adventurers are the primary heroes of the story, they are occasionally joined by NPC companions who grow and level alongside them. 

### Companion Types
* **Cohorts:** Unique companions tied to the Captain class and the Leadership feat. These companions benefit directly from the Captain's stats.
* **Hirelings:** Retainers ranging from simple commoners to trained combatants. They generally require payment for their ongoing services.
* **Sidekicks:** NPCs recruited along the party's journey, ranging from an awakened cat to a prisoner kobold.

### Experience and Challenge Rating
Bringing companions into battle adjusts the overall Challenge Rating (CR) of the encounter, but they do not penalize the party's experience gain. Instead, they earn an equal share of the experience point pool. For example, if experience is split three ways between the player characters, a sidekick or cohort will also earn that exact same value.

## Companion Classes

When a companion joins the party, they can be assigned one of the following classes to govern their progression.

| Class Name | Description | Prerequisites | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Warrior** | A companion that grows in martial prowess as it fights alongside you, such as a soldier, town guard, or battle-trained beast. | None | Core Rules |
| **Undead** | A silent, obedient undead thrall, such as a zombie, ghoul, or revenant, raised through necromancy. | Any Undead | Valda's Spire of Secrets |
| **Templar** | A daring knight sworn to virtue and station, knowing no fear in their heart and no equal in battle. | Any Humanoid | Valda's Spire of Secrets |
| **Steed** | A faithful mount that serves as a reliable, long-term adventuring companion. | None | Kibbles' Compendium |
| **Spellcaster** | A companion with magic in their veins, such as a hedge wizard, priest, soothsayer, or magical performer. | Must have at least one language in its stat block that it can speak. | Core Rules |
| **Scoundrel** | A stealthy and subversive companion who is as adept an assassin as they are a burglar or thief. | Any Humanoid | Valda's Spire of Secrets |
| **Mage** | A brilliant and studious spellcaster well-versed in the study of all things arcane. | Any Humanoid | Valda's Spire of Secrets |
| **Hunter** | A naturally skilled tracker and outlander comfortable in the depths of the woods. | Any Humanoid | Valda's Spire of Secrets |
| **Expert** | A master of tasks and knowledge who favors cunning over brawn. | Must have at least one language in its stat block that it can speak. | Core Rules |
| **Doggo** | A unique sidekick class to represent only the bestest of traveling companions. | Typically limited to doggos, or creatures considered a doggo by their companions. | KCLL |
