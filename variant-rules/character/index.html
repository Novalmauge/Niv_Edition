Ability Score Choices

You may choose one of the following methods to generate your six ability scores.

Generation Method Details
Min-Max Array 18, 14, 12, 8, 8, 8
Specialist Array 17, 15, 13, 12, 10, 8
Generalist Array 15, 14, 14, 14, 12, 12
Point Buy 27 points to spend (Minimum score 8, Maximum score 17 before species bonuses).
Old School Roll Roll 4d6, drop the lowest die. Do this six times. Then assign the scores to your.

!!! info "DM's Thoughts: Rolling vs. Reliability" I include the 'Old School Roll' with a swap option because discovering your character's strengths through the dice, rather than engineering them, can lead to highly memorable roleplay. However, Point Buy or an Array are highly recommended if you prefer a tightly balanced build.

Starting Gear & Money

When creating a character, you must decide how you acquired your starting equipment. Choose one of the following paths:

  • Standard Issue: Take the starting equipment packages granted by your class and background. Note* you may sell items for 50% the value from this to gain some additional starter gold.
  • Starting Wealth: Forgo the equipment packages entirely and roll for starting gold based on your class table in the Player's Handbook (Chapter 5). You must purchase all weapons, armor, and packs from this pool.
  • Bonus Trinket: Regardless of your choice above, every character begins with one item rolled randomly from the Gothic Trinkets table.

This changes when you start at a level higher than 4th.

  • 5th-8th level = 500gp plus 1d10x25gp & Normal Starting Equipment
  • 9th-12th level = 5000gp plus 1d10x250gp, two uncommon items, & Normal Starting Equipment
  • 13th-16th level =12000gp plus 1d10x250gp, two uncommon items, one rare item, & Normal Starting Equipment
  • 17th-20th level = 20,000gp plus 1d10×250gp, three uncommon magic items, two rare items, one very rare item, normal starting equipment

!!! info "DM's Thoughts: The Value of Scarcity" I lean heavily into the resource management aspect of the game. Choosing to roll for starting wealth is a gamble—you might afford premium armor, or you might start with just a dagger and a backpack. Scarcity drives early-game motivation, making your first major treasure haul feel incredible.
