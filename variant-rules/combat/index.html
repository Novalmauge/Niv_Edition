Combat Modifications

We use a few variant rules to speed up encounters and reward tactical positioning.

Actions in Combat

There are some default actions that almost everyone can do. Here is a list with decriptions.

Standard Action

  • Casting a spell: Characters with the spellcasting feature can choose to cast a spell.
  • Attack (melee, unarmed, or ranged): Anyone can choose to attack on their turn, martial classes often get to take multiple swings when they choose to attack.
  • Use a consumable: Most consumables have additional effects when used with an action, but can also be used as a bonus action.
  • Activate a Magic Item: Unless otherwise stated, magic items take an action to activate.
  • Dodge: Take a defensive stance, causing enemies to attack with disadvantage and allows player to roll dexterity saves with advantage
  • Dash: Allows you to move your entire speed an additional time this round
  • Disengage: Allows you to move your entire speed without provoking an attack of opportunity

Movement

  • Shift: A creature may choose to move shift 5-feet without provoking attacks of opportunity. This consumes all the movement on the turn and does not count as normal movement.
  • Run: If a creature forgo's it's standard and bonus action for the round, they move 4 times their movement speed in a straight line. They do however lose their dexterity bonus for any attacks of opportunity attacks that are provided during this action.
  • Move: Each creature may move up to it's speed per turn, using what ever appropriate movement type at the time.

Reaction

  • Attack of Opportunity: When a creature leaves an area threatened by another, it provokes an attack of opportunity which allows a single attack with a melee or armed attack.

Potions as a Bonus Action

  • Drinking a potion yourself: You may use a Standard Action to gain the full amount from a potion, or a bonus action to roll for HP.
  • Administering a potion to an ally: Requires a Standard Action.

Modified Flanking

Instead of granting advantage (which often invalidates class features and spells), flanking grants a +2 bonus to melee attack rolls against the flanked creature.

Find Familiar

When you Cast a Spell with a range of touch, or cast a cantrip, your familiar can deliver the spell as if it had cast the spell. Your familiar must be within 100 feet of you, and it must use its Reaction to deliver the spell when you cast it. If the spell requires an Attack roll, you use your Attack modifier for the roll.

Two-weapon Fighting and the Nick Mastery

When taking the attack action, you may take a single free attack with a different weapon that you're holding in the other hand instead of requiring a bonus action. All other two-weapon fighting rules apply. For example, must be light weapons without the two-weapon fighting feat, and the offhand doesn't apply the modifier to damage.

Unarmed strikes count for monks, unarmed fighting style fighters, puglists, and dance bards, and they may make one extra off-hand attack without spending a bonus action. Flurry of blows will still take a bonus action + a ki point to increase the number of bonus attacks from 1 to two.

Nick Weapon Mastery - Mastery with a weapon that has the Nick Property will give you one additional attack as part of the Attack action with that weapon

Example: A 5th fighter wielding two scimitars(nick) who takes the Attack action would strike four times. Twice from the Extra Attack feature, once from wielding a light weapon in their off-hand, and once from the Nick Property weapon mastery. Leaving the bonus action for something else, such as a scimitar of speed's bonus action attack, a potion, or a class feature.
